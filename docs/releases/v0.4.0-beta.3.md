# Viva 0.4.0-beta.3

[English](v0.4.0-beta.3.md) · [简体中文](v0.4.0-beta.3.zh-CN.md)

This Viva 0.4.0 beta adds asking about incoming messages directly in conversation, moves Background into the sidebar, and fixes cards and desktop-island layout.

## Changes

- **Read messages in conversation:** ask about today’s email or database alerts. Sources not fetched within one minute refresh first, waiting at most 20 seconds. Filter by source, keywords, and time; newest messages come first and are labeled as third-party data. Read-only, without an approval card. Credentials and databases stay in the app rather than being passed to the model.
- **Background in the sidebar:** alongside Search, About you, and Settings.
- **Simpler home:** a small cat head above the greeting replaces the large cat illustration, emphasizing greeting and input.
- **Fixes:** background cards stay under the message where they began instead of above the input; cards from other conversations do not appear in the current one. Deleting an item clears its right-hand detail. Dragging the desktop island no longer clips its sides.

## Verification recorded for this release

- macOS and Windows GitHub runners passed type checks, all tests, five smoke phases, packaging, silent installation, installed-app smoke, and uninstall.
- A complete physical-Windows check of notifications, tray, startup, sleep/wake, and 4K displays had not yet been performed. Please report issues.
